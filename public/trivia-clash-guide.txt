Welcome to Trivia Clash

How to play
1. Open the app and choose a subject session.
2. Answer as many questions correctly as you can before the timer ends.
3. Earn coins and cash rewards for correct answers.
4. Use coins to unlock hints or buy game packs.

Rewards
- Correct answers can add to your cash balance.
- Complete a full quiz to earn bonus rewards.
- Use the in-app store to buy coin packs.

Support
- For support, contact the game operator or admin.
- M-Pesa payments are simulated for demo purposes in the app.

Tips
- Keep your phone number ready when making a purchase.
- Review the daily subject schedule to catch each time slot.
- Practice in the free mode before entering paid tournaments.

Thank you for playing Trivia Clash!
